Colorstone Book Storefront Series

This project was a collaboration with Colorstone Book, a well-known Myanmar stationery brand. They discovered my storefront illustrations on social media and approached me to turn them into a series of book covers for their Watercolour Block line. The project gained a lot of attention online, leading to a full series launch that started with my designs. Ad Post · Painting Process Video · Watercolour Pastel Set Promotional Video

Better Learning Programme Comic

This comic was created in collaboration with Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC) for their Better Learning Programme in Myanmar. They provided story texts in multiple languages, which I adapted into illustrated comics, incorporating both visuals and text. The comic follows the journey of a child learning to cope after being displaced by military conflict. It was printed and distributed as part of NRC's refugee support efforts.
