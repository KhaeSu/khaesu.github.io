Better Learning Programme Comic

This comic was created in collaboration with Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC) for their Better Learning Programme in Myanmar. They provided story texts in multiple languages, which I adapted into illustrated comics, incorporating both visuals and text. The comic follows the journey of a child learning to cope after being displaced by military conflict. It was printed and distributed as part of NRC's refugee support efforts.

Better Learning Programme Poster

This educational poster was created to promote healthy routines, positive discipline, and emotional wellbeing for children affected by the conflict in Myanmar. The illustrations were designed to be clear, engaging, and culturally relevant, with key messages incorporated in multiple local languages provided by the organisation to ensure accessibility across different communities.

Information, Counselling and Legal Assistance (ICLA) Visual Materials

As part of NRC's ICLA programme in Myanmar, I developed visual materials including posters and leaflets to help share essential legal information with affected communities. The project involved transforming around 50 key messages into simple, easy-to-understand illustrations. To reach as many people as possible, the materials were produced in Burmese, English, and several regional languages.
