A little about me 🌱

Hello! I'm Khae, a Burmese artist currently based in Paris. I spend my days painting outdoors, biking through the city with my bike Noe, and following whatever small thing catches my eye, a shadow, a fallen leaf, the way the light hits a wall.

I studied at École Émile Cohl in Lyon, where I deepened my love for visual storytelling, traditional media, and the French language. These days, I work mostly with gouache, drawn to soft colors, greenery, flowers, and peaceful, fleeting moments. My work is often about the quiet details that feel important, even if we can't quite explain why.

I'm a deep feeler and a little impulsive, happiest when I'm collecting things I love: scraps, receipts, pressed flowers, memories. I journal a lot (I forget things easily) and collect notebooks obsessively, filling them with thoughts, sketches, and pieces of my days.

I love music that makes me feel everything, movies that make me cry, and solo cinema trips. I find comfort in simple things, nature, Studio Ghibli films, the warmth of green, and the soft magic hidden in everyday life.
